— 002   The Filmmaker

The
Filmmaker

A filmmaker and actor, currently based in Bengaluru. Aryan studied visual media and film making at St. Joseph's University, Bengaluru, where he worked on multiple student film projects and ad films, under different roles such as Director, Actor, Writer, Art Director, Cinematographer and Editor.

He has also shot social media content for brands and collaborated with social media consultants.

Currently looking for projects in visual media productions.
